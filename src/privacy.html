Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Empowered Health Wound and Foot Care ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit www.ehwoundandfootcare.com.

Information we collect on this website

This website is informational. It has no contact forms, no user accounts, and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to keep the site secure and running.

Phone, fax and email

If you call, fax or email us at admin@ehwoundandfootcare.com, we use your message only to respond to you and schedule care. Please do not send medical details by email; we will discuss your health privately during your visit.

Your health information

Health information you share as a patient is protected under applicable law, including HIPAA, and is kept in our medical records systems, not on this website.

Sale or sharing of information

We do not sell or share information collected through this website.

Children

This website is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

This site links to services we do not operate, such as Facebook and Instagram. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
