Jonathan Kamar

DMSc-S, PA-C, MHS, EM-CAQ, RT · Chief Administrative Officer

Jonathan Kamar is a nationally board-certified physician assistant, dually licensed, with specialized expertise in Emergency Medicine, Addiction Medicine, and Wound Care.

Read more

He earned his Bachelor of Science in Radiography from East Tennessee State University (ETSU) and his Master of Health Science in Physician Assistant Studies from South College. He is currently completing his Doctorate of Medical Science (DMSc) with a clinical concentration in Emergency Medicine at Lincoln Memorial University (LMU). Mr. Kamar serves as the Assistant Medical Director at a local Emergency Department, President and CEO of Total Recovery Medical Center, and Chief Administrative Officer for Empowered Health Wound & Foot Care. Deeply committed to serving the community where he was born and raised, he is passionate about advancing healthcare access and quality across East Tennessee.

Outside of his professional endeavors, Mr. Kamar enjoys spending time with his wife and two children, who he considers his greatest blessings. His passions extend beyond medicine to entrepreneurship and exploring the great outdoors.

Heath Tunnell

FNP-BC, CWCN-AP, WCS-C, EDS-C, WOCN · Chief Executive Officer

Heath Tunnell is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP), board-certified Wound, Ostomy, and Continence Nurse (WOCN), and owner and provider of Empowered Health Wound and Foot Care in Knoxville, Tennessee.

Read more

With specialized training in wound and foot care from Emory University, he has been practicing in this field for over a decade, delivering advanced, compassionate treatment for chronic wounds, diabetic foot issues, ulcers, and more.

As the founder of Empowered Health, Heath also integrates his wound and foot care expertise into holistic primary care, emphasizing root-cause solutions, personalized plans, and patient empowerment beyond traditional medicine. His evidence-based, approachable style has earned him outstanding reviews as one of Knoxville's top providers. He offers in-person visits in Knoxville and telemedicine across Tennessee for wound consultations, foot care, preventive health, and chronic management.

When he isn't practicing, Heath enjoys exploring the Great Smoky Mountains, staying active, and spending quality time with his wife and 4 children.
